============================================================
MAVLINK CONVERSION & FLIGHT BEST PRACTICES (ANAFI AI)
============================================================

1. THE "INDEX 0" COMMAND (TAKEOFF)
   - Ensure Index 0 is MAV_CMD_NAV_TAKEOFF (22).
   - The drone registers its "Home Point" at the EXACT moment 
     of liftoff, not when the script starts.
   - All mission altitudes are relative to this "0m" ground level.

2. PRE-FLIGHT GPS FIX (CRITICAL)
   - NEVER trigger a MAVLink mission until the drone reports 
     a "Fixed" GPS status (GPS_FIX_3D). 
   - If you takeoff with a weak lock, your Home Point (and 
     therefore your RTH) could be offset by several meters.

3. COORDINATE PRECISION & FORMATTING
   - Limit Lat/Lon to 7 decimal places.
   - Use MAV_FRAME_GLOBAL_RELATIVE_ALT (3) for all movement.
   - Use MAV_FRAME_MISSION (2) only for non-spatial commands 
     (like photo triggers).

4. THE "PHOTO COMMAND" (2000) LIMITATION
   - Command 2000 (IMAGE_START_CAPTURE) is often ignored if 
     the drone is moving too fast or in the wrong camera mode.
   - Better Practice: Use an Olympe 'observer' to trigger 
     photos via SDK when a waypoint index is reached.

5. RTH & FINISH ACTIONS
   - Command 20 (RETURN_TO_LAUNCH) uses the GPS coordinate 
     recorded at takeoff.
   - If the landing area is tight, consider using 
     MAV_CMD_NAV_LAND (21) for a precision landing at specific 
     coordinates instead of a general RTH.

6. SPHINX SIMULATION
   - Always run your converted .MAVLink file through the 
     Parrot Sphinx simulator first. If the virtual drone 
     skips a command, the physical one will too.
